# RECIPES

## Sheet‑pan chicken thighs with 3‑ingredient pan sauce
@testchef - 2026-08-20 - https://www.instagram.com/p/XXXXXXXXXX/

**seriouseats.com**

**TL;DR:** Crispy sheet‑pan chicken thighs finished with a quick vinegar‑Dijon‑butter sauce.

**Steps:**
1. Dry the skin of the chicken thighs
2. Salt the thighs and let sit uncovered for 40 minutes
3. Preheat oven to 220 °C and place a sheet pan on the top rack
4. Roast the thighs for ~25 minutes until skin is golden and crisp
5. Remove the thighs, add a splash of vinegar to the hot pan, stir in 1 tsp Dijon mustard and a knob of butter to create a pan sauce
6. Pour the sauce over the chicken and serve

**Key points:**
- Dry the chicken skin thoroughly before cooking
- Salt the thighs and let them rest uncovered for 40 minutes
- Roast on the top rack at 220 °C for about 25 minutes until skin is crisp
- Deglaze the pan with a splash of vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, and a knob of butter for a simple pan sauce
- Serve the thighs with the pan sauce drizzled over

**Mentioned:** chicken thighs, vinegar, Dijon mustard, butter

**Verbatim:**
```
220 °C
40 min
1 tsp Dijon
```
